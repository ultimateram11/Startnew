EagleEye AI

Adaptive Search & Rescue Intelligence System

Inspired by how birds scan large areas from above. Multiple autonomous agents search a virtual disaster area while a live probability heatmap is updated from every observation, answering one question: where should the team look next?

Priority = Probability + Uncertainty + Unexplored − Cost
Explore→Evidence→Update Map→Prioritize↺
Live demo: agents sweep, and the map cools where areas are searched.
